# 🌍 ServeLink

### *Connecting People to Purpose, One Act of Kindness at a Time*

---

## 🚀 Overview

ServeLink is a **data-driven volunteer coordination platform** that bridges the gap between **people who want to help** and **organizations that need support**.

From old age homes and orphanages to NGOs and healthcare centers — we make it easy to **discover nearby opportunities, respond to real community needs, and organize collective action**.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Community needs are everywhere — but information about them is often:

* Scattered across social media, word of mouth, or internal NGO records
* Hard for volunteers to discover
* Difficult to prioritize or act on quickly

At the same time, many people **want to help but don’t know where to start**.

---

## ✨ Our Solution

ServeLink creates a **centralized, location-based platform** where:

* NGOs can share needs effortlessly
* Volunteers can find nearby opportunities instantly
* Communities can highlight and prioritize real-world issues

We bring together **location, data, and community collaboration** to make volunteering smarter and more impactful.

---

## 🔥 Core Features

### 📍 1. Discover NGOs Near You

* Interactive map powered by Google Maps
* View organizations around your current location
* Filter by tags like *Old Age Home, Orphanage, Healthcare*

---

### 📋 2. Volunteer Job Listings

* Browse tasks like:

  * Meal distribution 🍱
  * Maintenance work 🔧
  * Caretaking ❤️
* Includes deadlines and requirements
* Supports **auto-imported listings from NGO websites**

---

### 🤖 3. Smart Listing Aggregation

* Automatically pulls volunteer opportunities from NGO websites
* Reduces manual effort for organizations
* Redirects users to official NGO pages to apply

---

### 📸 4. Community Issue Reporting

* Volunteers can report real-world issues
* Upload images for verification
* Tag the relevant organization

Example:
“Broken wheelchair ramp at Karunalayam”

---

### 👍 5. Upvote-Based Prioritization

* Community can upvote issues
* Most urgent problems rise to the top
* Ensures attention goes where it’s needed most

---

### 💬 6. Community Discussion & Event Coordination

* Each issue has a discussion thread
* Volunteers can:

  * Plan visits
  * Coordinate group efforts
  * Organize events

Example:
“Let’s go this Saturday at 10 AM”

---

## 🧠 Why This Matters

ServeLink is not just a platform — it’s a **community engine**.

* 🧭 Helps volunteers find meaningful work nearby
* 🏥 Supports NGOs with better visibility
* 📊 Organizes scattered community data
* ⚡ Enables faster response to urgent needs

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** React
* **Backend:** Node.js + Express
* **Database:** Firebase / JSON (prototype)
* **Maps:** Google Maps API
